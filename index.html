VOXEL DRIVER

An off-road expedition across a procedurally generated voxel island. Twelve landmarks are out there — in the snowfields up north, the desert down south, on the peaks and along the coast. Follow the waypoint arrow, pick your own line over the terrain, and chart the whole island. Roads link the landmarks and are quicker; side trails break off to quiet dead ends.
