VOXEL DRIVER

An off-road expedition across an endless, procedurally generated voxel world: grassland, forest, desert, snowfields, mountains, seas and cities, with no edge to reach. There are fourteen kinds of place to find, and always more of them further out. Follow the waypoint arrow or pick your own line. Roads link every place and are quicker; side trails break off to quiet dead ends.

W A S D / arrowsDrive and steer SpaceHandbrake (drift) ShiftBoost — fast enough and you plough through trees and bounce over rocks EHop. In the air, A / D spins, and Space with W / S flips. Red mushrooms are trampolines. BWings. Glide and fly: S pulls up, W dives, A / D bank into a turn, Shift is a push, Space is the airbrake. B again folds them. G / right clickIn flight: drop a glitter bomb. Whatever it lands on gets yassified: the ground, the trees, the buildings, and any thief or hunter standing there. F / clickGrappling hook. Hit a thief to reel it in or a hunter to stun it. Miss, and it drags you to wherever it lands.
